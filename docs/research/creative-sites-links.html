Creative personal sites — investigation index

Tip: links you've already visited fade to gray, so you can work through the list.

Deep-dived canonical sites

The five sites the research verified in depth — start here.

  • bruno-simon.com — Bruno SimonSOTD 2019 · Site of the Month Jan 2026

    Navigation is a drivable 3D car world — no menu exists. Achievements with unlockable skins, a daily top-10 leaderboard, a 30-character multiplayer "Whispers" wall, and jokes in functional UI copy ("I'm stuck!"). The maximalist end of discovery-as-interface.

    Also: his case study · Awwwards page (rebuild)

  • joshwcomeau.com — Josh Comeauclosest architectural match

    Whimsy layered on a readable editorial blog. Cursor-reactive rainbow hero with a configurator that appears only on hover — and syncs live to every other visitor via WebSockets. MDX posts embed custom interactive components; same stack family as frhd.me.

    Also: How I Built My Blog v2

  • lynnandtonic.com — Lynn Fishereditorial + one committed metaphor

    The site is a printed paperback: fixed 436px book column, paper texture (dust in dark mode), chapter headers. Its signature easter egg appears only when you resize the window — the page stretches elastically and snaps back. Famous for total annual redesigns.

    Also: 2025 refresh case study · Rach Smith's tour of her past sites

  • gwern.net — Gwern Branwentypography reference

    A reading machine: Tufte-style sidenotes in the margins, three visually distinct link-underline styles, recursive preview popups — all progressive enhancement over a core that needs no JavaScript. Maps directly onto a static-export site.

    Also: Design of This Website · the sidenotes essay

  • cassie.codes — Cassie Evansplayful SVG/motion

    Interactive animated SVG self-portrait and playful GSAP motion throughout — personality through animation on an otherwise readable base.

    Also: Making a lil' me
