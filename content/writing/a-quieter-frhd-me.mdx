---
title: "a quieter frhd.me"
date: "2026-07-18"
summary: "why this site is plain text now"
---

for a while this page was a whole computer. frhd.me booted into an xterm.js
terminal: you typed `help`, poked around a fake filesystem, played tetris,
edited files in a tiny vim. i loved building it and it was a fun
thing to land on.

but a homepage should say things plainly. if you come here to find out what i
work on or read something i wrote, you shouldn't have to guess a command
first. so the front of the site is just text now: a short intro, a few
projects, whatever i've been writing, and how to reach me.

i didn't delete the terminal. it was too much fun to throw away, and it's still
the truest thing about how i like to work. it just moved. it lives at
`/terminal` now, or you can press `t` from anywhere on the site and it'll take
you straight there.

the whole thing is a static next.js export: no server, no database, nothing
to keep running. plain files, quietly.
